General Rules
Defeat the boss by reducing its hp to 0 in a certain amount of allotted time (10 rounds)
stamina is the currency used to take actions
boss attacks are “reactions” on certain conditions

Gameplay
Boss Mechanics
The boss will be at the center of the board and will rotate to face heroes. Each boss will have their own specific mechanics, but here are the general ones.

Every boss will have exposed sides and protected sides. You can only damage the boss on its exposed sides, and similarly, you cannot take an attack action on a boss’s protected sides.
Setup
Each hero starts off by choosing their starting sector. The boss starts off facing north.

In every round, each hero will take their turn in order. The player with the aggro token goes first each round, then play proceeds in order. The boss will not rotate towards the player with the aggro token after setup.

The fortitude hero starts off with the aggro token, then the order is:
fortitude, wisdom, courage, unity, creativity

A turn consists of 2 phases: movement phase → action phase
Movement Phase 
choose whether you want to spend 1 stamina to move to an adjacent sector (e.g. hero in north sector can move to either east or west sector)
if you choose not to move, you will become threatened
Action Phase
spend stamina to take actions

Drawing Aggro
Certain actions will draw aggro. There is one aggro token that is passed amongst heroes. When you take an action that draws aggro, take the aggro token. After all heroes take their action, the boss will rotate to face the player with the aggro token. Then this player will start the new round by taking their turn.
Block
Some actions will allow you to gain block, which will reduce the damage taken from the boss (e.g. if the boss does 5 damage, and you have 2 block, you will lose your 2 block, and then difference in damage, so you will lose 3 hp).

All block gained will last until the end of the hero’s next turn.
Player one gains 2 block on his turn
Player two gives player one 2 block. Player one now has 4 block
The boss reaction at the end of the round deals 2 damage. Player one blocks this damage, resulting in only having 2 block.
At the start of player one’s next turn, they trigger a boss reaction, taking 1 damage. Player one now has 1 block.
Player one gains 3 block on his turn
At the end of player one’s turn, he loses the 1 block he had from the previous round, resulting in player one having 3 block at the end of their turn.
Boss Reactions
Boss attacks are reactions that will trigger off of heroes’ actions. When a reaction is triggered, it must be immediately resolved. If multiple reactions occur, the hero chooses the order to resolve them in.

If a hero action causes the boss’s hp to reach 0, do not resolve any reactions.
End of Round
After all heroes have taken their turn and all reactions have been resolved:
Recover all stamina
Begin the new turn
Knocked Out (KO)
When you reach 0 hp or lower, you are knocked out. When knocked out, you instantly end your turn, lose all statuses (e.g. threatened, burned), then on the next turn, you revive with half hp, lose 1 max stamina, and then end your turn.

Note: The -1 max stamina penalty stacks, so on your first KO, you will have 2 max stamina, then on your second KO, you will have 1 max stamina, and so on.



First Boss:
Concept: Some sort of fire-breathing dragon

Exposed Sectors: Front, Rear
Protected Sectors: Flanks
Reactions:
If a hero starts their movement phase in the front sector, take 5 damage.
If a threatened hero starts their movement phase in the front sector, take 3 additional damage (8 total damage), and become burned
burned - on next turn, cannot attack, and lose 1 hp
After every hero has taken their turn, the boss gains 1 struggle.
If the boss reaches 3 struggle, all heroes take 3 damage, all threatened heroes take 2 additional damage and become burned
